# Changelog

## 0.3 (2026-11-02)

- Sounds: gravel crunching under a rolling loaded truck, clanks from an empty one and
  when loading or unloading, a creak when it's swung round hard (`SoundVolume`).
- Tied-up players (tape, rope, handcuffs) can be loaded with F onto an empty truck and
  set down with a click. They ride upright on the nose plate, the truck leaning back
  `PersonTiltDegrees`, and get off by themselves when freed, killed, knocked out or the
  truck leaves the pusher's hands (`AllowPeople`).
- Settings file version 9 (`SoundVolume`, `AllowPeople`, `PersonTiltDegrees` get their
  defaults).

## 0.2

- Life Machines add-on (stove and sewing machine), loot entries shipped in
  `extras/types_handcart.xml`, first Workshop release.

## 0.1

- The hand truck: loads, unloading with a ghost, vehicles, barrels, damage looks.
