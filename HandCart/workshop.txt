A hand truck (sack truck) for DayZ. Wheel heavy things around instead of carrying them one at a time.

[h2]What it carries[/h2]
One kind of load at a time, stacked, contents and all (a full barrel or sea chest stays full):
[list]
[*] Sea chests: 2
[*] Wooden crates: 4
[*] MS3 dual wheel: 1, MS3 or M1025 wheels: 2, car wheels: 3
[*] Power generator: 1
[*] Canopy tents, packed: 3
[*] Barrels and fire barrels: 1 (the lid goes on; a fire barrel must be out)
[*] A person tied up with tape, rope or handcuffs: 1 (they ride standing on the nose plate)
[*] Expansion safes, locked or not (door shut): 1, with [b]Hand Truck - Expansion Safes[/b]
[*] Life Machines' stove and sewing machine: 1, with [b]Hand Truck - Life Machines[/b]
[/list]

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*] Hold it and you push it in front of you, tilted back on its wheels. Empty, you can jog; loaded, you walk, slower off-road. No sprinting, jumping, climbing or vaulting with it.
[*] It steers like a real one: push it forward or pull it back (no sidestepping), turn slowly with the mouse or A and D, and it pivots on its wheels while you walk the handles round. It tips and rolls with the ground. Gravel crunches under a loaded truck; an empty one clanks.
[*] Load: look at something and press F, or drag it onto the truck in your inventory.
[*] Unload: click for a ghost of the top item, aim it (mouse wheel or Z/X to turn), hold to set it down within 2 m. Or drag it off in the inventory.
[*] People: holding an empty truck, look at someone tied up and press F. Click to set them down where they stand. They get off by themselves if they're freed, killed or knocked out, or if the truck is dropped.
[*] Vehicles: F at a vehicle offers to load from it (its slots, like an MS3's barrel and crate slots, then its cargo) or put the top item in. Dragging works too.
[*] The truck itself fits in any vehicle's cargo.
[*] Blue, red and yellow, with damaged and ruined looks.
[/list]

[h2]For server owners[/h2]
[b]Install[/b]
Paths are relative to the server's install folder (the one with DayZServer_x64.exe or DayZServer), shown here as DayZServer/.
[olist]
[*] Put the mod's folder in the server's install folder as DayZServer/@HandCart (with its subfolders addons/, keys/ and extras/).
[*] Add it to the server's launch parameter, after your other mods: -mod=...;@HandCart
With the add-ons, they go straight after it: -mod=...;@HandCart;@HandCart_Expansion;@HandCart_LifeMachines
[*] Copy the key file DayZServer/@HandCart/keys/tarbaby.bikey into DayZServer/keys/ (next to dayz.bikey). Without it, players using the mod are kicked for unsigned files. The two add-ons are signed with the same key, tarbaby.bikey, so this one file covers them too.
[*] Copy the loot entries DayZServer/@HandCart/extras/types_handcart.xml into a folder of your mission, for example DayZServer/mpmissions/dayzOffline.chernarusplus/handcart/types_handcart.xml (or into a folder you already use for modded types).
[*] Register that file in your mission's DayZServer/mpmissions/dayzOffline.chernarusplus/cfgeconomycore.xml, inside <economycore>, using the folder name from step 4:
[code]
<ce folder='handcart'>
    <file name='types_handcart.xml' type='types' />
</ce>
[/code]
If you used a folder that already has a <ce> block, add just the <file ... /> line to it.
[*] Restart the server.
[/olist]
Players only need to subscribe; the DayZ launcher loads the mod for them.

The loot entries add three colours, about 9 hand trucks in all, spawning where power generators do (tools, Industrial) but only at floor level, never on shelves. Instead of steps 4 and 5 you can paste the three <type> entries from types_handcart.xml into your mission's db/types.xml.

[b]Settings[/b]
The server writes [i]$profile:HandCart/HandCartSettings.json[/i] with defaults on its first start and sends it to every client. Edit it and restart.
[list]
[*] MaxUnloadDistance (2): metres from the player to where an item is set down
[*] MaxSlopeDegrees (30): steepest ground an item may be set down on
[*] RequireLineOfSight (true): no unloading through walls
[*] UnloadTime (1.5): seconds to set an item down
[*] SoftGroundSpeed (0.6): loaded walking speed off-road (1 = normal walk)
[*] HoldTiltDegrees, HoldGripForward, HoldLift (50, 0.35, 0.7): how the held truck sits in front of the player
[*] SteerLikeACart (true): holding a truck, forward and back only, with slow turns that pivot on its wheels
[*] TurnRateDegrees (60): fastest a player holding a truck turns, in degrees a second
[*] FollowTerrain (true): the held truck tips and rolls with the ground
[*] SoundVolume (1): the truck's rolling and clanking (0 = off)
[*] AllowPeople (true): tied-up players can be loaded
[*] PersonTiltDegrees (15): how far a truck carrying a person leans back
[*] HardSurfaces, SoftSurfaces (empty): surface names to always treat as road, or as dirt
[*] BlockedItems (empty): class names (and their subclasses) that may never be loaded
[/list]
