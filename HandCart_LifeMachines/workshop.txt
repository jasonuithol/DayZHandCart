Add-on for [b]Hand Truck[/b]: lets the hand truck carry the stove and sewing machine from Life Machines (huntermods.online), which otherwise can't be moved once deployed. Contents ride along; not while they're working. They can also go into a vehicle's cargo. Nothing of Life Machines is repacked.

Requires: Hand Truck and Life Machines. Load it after both. Server and client: copy the key from its keys/ folder into your server keys/ folder.
